FAQ

Questions business owners ask me

How long does it take?

Starter sites usually take 1 to 2 weeks, Growth sites 2 to 4 weeks, and Premium builds 4 to 6 weeks. Timelines move fastest when you send photos and text promptly.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. Once the final payment is made, the design, code and domain are yours. I never lock you in.

What about hosting?

I set up fast, reliable hosting for you. You can pay the host directly (usually $10 to $25 a month) or have it handled in the Monthly Care Plan.

How many revisions are included?

Starter includes 2 rounds, Growth and Premium include 3. Because you approve a design mockup first, most projects need far fewer.

Will I rank #1 on Google?

No honest developer can promise that. What I do is build a strong local SEO foundation: fast speed, clean structure, schema markup, service-area pages and Google Business Profile setup, so you have the best possible chance.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to reserve your spot and 50% when the site is approved, before it goes live. Care plans are billed monthly.
